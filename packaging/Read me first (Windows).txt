FFmpeg GUI - Media Converter for Windows

1. If you haven't already, right-click the zip and choose Extract All. You can move FFmpeg GUI - Media Converter.exe anywhere you like, such as your Desktop.

2. Double-click FFmpeg GUI - Media Converter.exe.
   The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't from a known publisher.
   Click More info, then Run anyway. It only asks once.

3. The app opens in its own window. If FFmpeg isn't installed yet, click Install FFmpeg and it sets itself up.

Updates: when a new version of Media Converter or FFmpeg is out, the app asks when you open it. Click Update now.
Converted files are saved next to the originals, or in a folder you choose. Originals are never changed.
To close the app, click Quit or just close its window.
