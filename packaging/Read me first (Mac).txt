Media Converter for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer)

1. Drag "Media Converter" into your Applications folder.

2. First time only: macOS blocks apps that aren't from the App Store or a registered developer.
   To allow this one, open Terminal, paste this line, and press Return:

      xattr -cr "/Applications/Media Converter.app"

   (Another way: double-click the app, click Done on the warning, then open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.)

3. Double-click Media Converter. It opens in its own window (in Chrome or Edge if you have one,
   otherwise in your normal browser). If FFmpeg isn't installed yet, click Install FFmpeg and
   it sets itself up.

If macOS asks whether Media Converter can access a folder such as Downloads or Documents,
click Allow so it can read your videos.

Updates: when a new version of Media Converter or FFmpeg is out, the app asks when you open it.
Click Update now. (Keep the app in Applications so it can update itself.)

Converted files are saved next to the originals, or in a folder you choose. Originals are never changed.
To close the app, click Quit or just close its window.
